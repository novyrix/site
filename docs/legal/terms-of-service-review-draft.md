# Novyrix Terms Of Service

Review copy for legal and operational review. This draft is not legal advice.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

## 1. Scope

These terms apply to the use of novyrix.com, Novyrix inquiry flows, private payment links, and any Novyrix client portal made available to an accepted client.

Project delivery, payment schedules, ownership terms, confidentiality terms, support responsibilities, acceptance criteria, and special compliance requirements may also be governed by a proposal, statement of work, master services agreement, data processing agreement, or another signed document. If there is a conflict, the signed engagement document controls for that engagement.

## 3. Inquiries And Discovery

Submitting an inquiry does not create a contract, guarantee availability, reserve capacity, or require Novyrix to accept the work.

Novyrix may review an inquiry, request more context, hold a discovery call, decline the work, or prepare a proposal. Novyrix may decline work that is outside its capability, outside its risk appetite, unclear in scope, legally sensitive without appropriate safeguards, or not a good fit for the current operating schedule.

## 4. Proposals And Engagements

An engagement begins only when Novyrix and the client agree to the required scope, commercial terms, approval process, and agreement reference.

Each proposal may define:

- Scope and exclusions.
- Milestones and deliverables.
- Timeline and dependencies.
- Currency and payment schedule.
- Client approvals and acceptance criteria.
- Support, handover, or maintenance terms.
- Special privacy, security, or compliance requirements.

Public pages may describe engagement models, but they do not publish a rate card. Project pricing is resolved through inquiry, discovery, scope, location, currency, and the accepted proposal.

## 5. Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for:

- Providing accurate project, business, legal, technical, and payment information.
- Confirming that the person approving the engagement has authority to do so.
- Giving timely access to systems, accounts, content, data, brand materials, decision makers, and third-party providers needed for the work.
- Reviewing drafts, milestone outputs, invoices, and deliverables within the agreed timeline.
- Ensuring that client-provided materials do not infringe third-party rights.
- Telling Novyrix about security, privacy, donor, government, financial, or regulatory requirements that may affect the work.
- Keeping portal credentials and private payment links secure.

Delays in feedback, access, approval, content, credentials, or third-party decisions may affect timelines.

## 6. Client Portal

Novyrix may provide a private portal for signed clients. The portal may show milestones, deliverables, invoice history, payment status, and a logged message channel.

Portal access is role-based and may be limited, suspended, or removed if access is no longer needed, if an account appears compromised, if an engagement ends, or if these terms or the relevant agreement are breached.

The client must not share passwords, publish private portal links, attempt to access another client's records, probe the service, upload unlawful material, or use the portal in a way that harms the security or reliability of Novyrix systems.

## 7. Invoices And Payments

Novyrix may issue itemized invoices through the portal, by email, or by private payment link. Each invoice should identify the work, line items, total, currency, due date, and payment status.

Payments may be processed through Paystack, BTCPay Server, bank transfer, or another agreed payment rail. Payment is treated as confirmed only after Novyrix or the relevant provider verifies settlement.

The client is responsible for applicable taxes, bank fees, card fees, mobile money fees, blockchain network fees, foreign exchange costs, and provider charges unless an accepted proposal says otherwise.

Chargebacks, reversals, failed payments, underpayments, overpayments, duplicate payments, or blockchain settlement issues may require manual reconciliation. Novyrix will keep a reasonable record of payment attempts, verified payments, receipts, and provider references.

## 8. Bitcoin And Lightning Payments

If a client chooses Bitcoin or Lightning payment, the client is responsible for sending the correct amount to the correct invoice during the valid payment window and for understanding network fees, confirmation timing, volatility, and wallet behaviour.

Novyrix does not ask for private keys, seed phrases, wallet backups, or custody credentials.

Unless an accepted proposal says otherwise, final project deliverables created specifically for the client are assigned or licensed to the client after the relevant fees are paid.

Novyrix keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, internal methods, generic know-how, reusable code patterns, documentation templates, design systems, deployment workflows, and background intellectual property. ## 10. Confidentiality

Each party may receive non-public information from the other. The receiving party should use confidential information only for the engagement and should protect it using reasonable care.

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is already public, independently developed, lawfully received from another source, or required to be disclosed by law or a competent authority.

Specific confidentiality, data protection, donor, government, or regulated-sector requirements should be written into the signed engagement document.
