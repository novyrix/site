# Novyrix Privacy Policy

Review copy for legal and operational review. This draft is not legal advice.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

## 1. Who This Policy Covers

This policy explains how Novyrix handles personal data when someone uses novyrix.com, submits an inquiry, communicates with Novyrix, reviews an invoice, pays through an approved payment rail, or uses a Novyrix client portal after an engagement is accepted.

Novyrix works with organisations that need secure software platforms, workflow automation, payment infrastructure, and technical delivery support. Because those projects often involve operational or programme data, Novyrix treats privacy and access control as part of delivery, not as an afterthought.

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact:

- spira@novyrix.com
- connect@novyrix.com
- contact@novyrix.com

## 2. Personal Data We May Collect

Novyrix may collect the following categories of personal data:

- Contact details, such as name, email address, organisation, country, role, and communication preferences.
- Inquiry details, such as services requested, project context, operational needs, budget range, location, and discovery notes.
- Engagement details, such as proposal references, agreement references, milestone records, deliverable links, project messages, invoice records, payment status, and receipt references.
- Portal account details, such as email address, name, account status, login activity, and client organisation membership.
- Payment records, such as invoice references, payment provider, payment status, transaction reference, receipt reference, amount, currency, and payment channel.
- Technical and security data, such as IP address, device and browser metadata, timestamps, request logs, error logs, security events, and uptime monitoring signals.

Novyrix does not intentionally collect full card numbers, card security codes, private Bitcoin keys, seed phrases, or wallet credentials. Card, M-Pesa, Bitcoin, and Lightning payment details are handled by the payment provider or payment server used for the transaction.

## 3. How Personal Data Is Collected

Personal data may be collected when:

- A visitor submits an inquiry or contacts Novyrix by email.
- Novyrix records discovery notes, fit decisions, proposal details, agreement confirmation, invoices, milestones, messages, and deliverables.
- A client uses the private portal or a private payment link.
- Payment providers send settlement, webhook, callback, or receipt events.
- Novyrix systems generate technical, monitoring, security, or audit logs.

## 4. Why Novyrix Uses Personal Data

Novyrix uses personal data only where there is a business, contractual, legal, security, or consent-based reason to do so. The main purposes are:

- To respond to inquiries and assess whether Novyrix is the right fit for a project.
- To prepare discovery notes, proposals, statements of work, invoices, receipts, and project records.
- To deliver agreed services and maintain a clear record of milestones, messages, approvals, and deliverables.
- To process payments through Paystack, BTCPay Server, or another approved provider.
- To protect Novyrix systems, client records, payment flows, and private access routes.
- To meet accounting, tax, legal, dispute-resolution, and audit obligations.
- To improve website reliability, accessibility, content quality, and operational workflows.

Novyrix does not sell personal data.

## 5. Client Portal Data

The Novyrix client portal is intended for signed clients only. It may show engagement status, milestones, deliverables, invoice history, payment status, and project messages.

Portal access is role-based. A lead does not receive portal access just because they submitted an inquiry. Portal access is provisioned after an agreement is accepted or when Novyrix has another legitimate reason to provide access.

Clients are responsible for keeping their portal credentials secure and for ensuring that the people they ask Novyrix to add are authorised to view the engagement information.

## 6. Payments

Novyrix may use Paystack for card and M-Pesa payments, and BTCPay Server for Bitcoin or Lightning payments. Payment confirmations may be received through provider callbacks, verification requests, or signed webhooks.

Novyrix stores the information needed to reconcile invoices and issue receipts. This can include invoice reference, payment attempt reference, provider reference, amount, currency, status, channel, and receipt reference.

Novyrix does not store full payment card details, card security codes, mobile money credentials, private keys, seed phrases, or wallet backup data.

## 7. Service Providers And Infrastructure

Novyrix may use carefully selected providers to operate the site, API, portal, email, payments, security, monitoring, backups, and private networking. Current or planned providers may include:

- Vercel for hosting the public site and portal frontend.
- A self-managed Linux VPS and PostgreSQL database for platform records.
- Cloudflare for DNS, tunneling, security, and access routing.
- Resend for transactional and operational email.
- Paystack for card and M-Pesa payment processing.
- BTCPay Server for Bitcoin and Lightning invoice processing.
- Uptime Kuma for uptime monitoring and service alerts.
- Tailscale or similar private networking for secure operational access.

Novyrix gives providers access only where needed for the service they perform.

## 8. International Transfers

Some providers or infrastructure may process or route data outside Kenya. Where this happens, Novyrix aims to use providers with appropriate security controls and to limit the data shared to what is necessary for the relevant service.

Where a project requires stricter data residency, donor compliance, government-adjacent handling, or client-specific data processing terms, those requirements should be addressed in the proposal, statement of work, data processing agreement, or another signed engagement document.

## 9. Security Measures

Novyrix uses technical and organisational controls designed to protect personal data, including private server access, database access restrictions, environment-variable separation, webhook verification, no-index rules for private routes, monitoring, backups, and access-control checks.

No online service can guarantee perfect security. If Novyrix becomes aware of a security issue that affects personal data, it will investigate and take appropriate steps based on the nature of the issue, the data involved, and applicable legal obligations.

## 10. Retention

Novyrix keeps personal data only as long as needed for the reason it was collected, including inquiry handling, project delivery, payment reconciliation, accounting, legal records, security, dispute resolution, and legitimate business continuity.

General inquiry records may be retained long enough to understand the commercial history and avoid repeated discovery work. Engagement, invoice, receipt, accounting, and legal records may be kept for longer where required for tax, audit, contractual, or dispute-resolution reasons.

When data is no longer needed, Novyrix will delete it, anonymise it, or restrict access where deletion is not immediately practical.

## 11. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to:

- Be informed about how your personal data is used.
- Access personal data held about you.
- Correct inaccurate or misleading personal data.
- Request deletion of data where appropriate.
- Object to certain processing.
- Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent.
- Request restriction of processing in appropriate circumstances.
- Request portability where applicable.
- Raise a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

To make a request, contact spira@novyrix.com, connect@novyrix.com, or contact@novyrix.com. Novyrix may need to verify your identity and authority before responding, especially where the request concerns client, project, invoice, or portal records.

## 12. Children

Novyrix services are for organisations and business contacts. The site, payment flows, and portal are not directed at children.

## 13. Changes To This Policy

Novyrix may update this policy as the site, portal, infrastructure, legal obligations, or providers change. The updated version will show a new "Last updated" date.
